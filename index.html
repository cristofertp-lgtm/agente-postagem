Agente YT

Agente YT is a personal desktop tool used by content creator Chris Ballardim to publish his own short-form gaming videos to his own accounts on TikTok, YouTube and Instagram.

How it works

  1. The creator selects a finished video stored on his own computer.
  2. The tool suggests a title, caption and hashtags, which the creator reviews and approves.
  3. After approval, the video is uploaded to the creator's own account through each platform's official API (for TikTok, the Content Posting API).

The tool is not offered to the public and does not access any other user's account or data.
