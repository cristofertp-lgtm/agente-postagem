Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Agente YT ("the app") handles information. The app is a personal tool operated by Chris Ballardim to publish videos to his own social media accounts.

Information we access

  • Account authorization: when the account owner connects TikTok, YouTube or Instagram, the app receives an access token and basic profile information (such as account ID and display name) to identify the connected account.
  • Content: the videos, titles, captions and hashtags that the account owner chooses to publish.

How information is used

Information is used only to upload and publish the account owner's videos to the account he connected, and to show the result of each upload (for example, the post status).

Storage

Access tokens are stored locally on the account owner's computer. The app does not operate a server or database, and does not store data from other users.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any information with third parties. Data is sent only to the platform where the owner chooses to publish (TikTok, YouTube or Instagram), through their official APIs.

Third-party services

Use of each platform is also subject to its own policies: TikTok Privacy Policy, Google Privacy Policy and Instagram Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time in the security or app settings of each platform. Locally stored tokens can be deleted at any time, and you may request deletion by contacting us at the email below.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

This policy may be updated. The effective date above will reflect the latest version.
